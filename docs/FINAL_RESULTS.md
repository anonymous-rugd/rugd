## Executive reading

RUGD leads the 11-model clean suite in macro-F1 (0.8381 +/- 0.0112) and severe AUPRC (0.8883 +/- 0.0310). Its AUPRC advantage over STAEformer-Inductive is +0.0068. STAEformer remains stronger under the two heaviest missing-sensor conditions, so the result is a lightweight clean-performance win rather than universal dominance.

## Research-integrity and publication status

The final specification and its SHA256 artifact manifest are retained under `outputs/studies/final_study/FINAL_STUDY_MANIFEST.json`. The three OpenCity folds are development evidence, not an independent geographic confirmation set.

The study is a credible research prototype with strong clean results, modern adapted comparisons, broad uncertainty/robustness analysis, and reproducible artifacts. A strong publication submission still needs preregistered evaluation on additional cities or an external dataset, ideally official baseline reproductions and more geographic units.
